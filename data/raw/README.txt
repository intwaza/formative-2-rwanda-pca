Season B: Agricultural Practices
================================

Summary: This table presents plot-level data on farming practices during Season B (March – June 2024) of the 2023/2024 Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) conducted by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. Each record represents a specific agricultural plot and the practices applied to it by the farming household during the reference season. The table includes information on soil erosion status, anti-erosion measures, land consolidation, use of mechanical equipment, hired labour expenditure, and irrigation practices such as techniques, water sources, and associated costs.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, including all provinces and districts, capturing agricultural practices in both rural and peri-urban areas.

Time Period: Season B of the 2023/2024 agricultural year, covering the period from March to June 2024.

Frequency: Seasonal (three times per year: Seasons A, B, and C).

Population/Units: Agricultural plots managed by small-scale and large-scale farming households in Rwanda.

Key Variables: Soil erosion status, anti-erosion practices, land consolidation, machinery use, hired labour costs, irrigation methods, water sources, and irrigation-related expenses.

Purpose: The table is designed to support analysis of land management practices, resource use, and farming investment at the plot and household level.

Data Quality Notes: The data reflect reported practices at the plot level for surveyed households and may not fully represent all farming activities nationwide. Coverage and reporting may vary across regions and respondents.
